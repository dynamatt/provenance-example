---
id: VER-0002
type: VerificationProtocol
title: Bench verification of single-fault amplitude ceiling
steps:
  - description: |-
      Connect the implant's sense electrode through the fault-injection lead
      simulator, with its current monitor on the stimulation lead. Enable
      closed-loop control with the amplitude at its configured maximum.
    acceptance_criteria: |-
      The current monitor reads the configured maximum amplitude, within
      ± 2 %.
  - description: |-
      Through the test interface, command an amplitude of 10 mA, above the
      ceiling.
    acceptance_criteria: Delivered amplitude does not exceed 8 mA.
  - description: |-
      Disconnect the sense electrode during active stimulation, hold for 30
      seconds and reconnect. Repeat for ten trials.
    acceptance_criteria: |-
      Delivered amplitude does not exceed 8 mA at any time in any of the ten
      trials.
status: approved
verifies:
  - REQ-0002
device_under_test:
  - CMP-0001
equipment_needed:
  - EQP-0003
---
Simulate sense-electrode lead disconnection during active stimulation and
confirm delivered amplitude never exceeds 8 mA, exercising the hardware
current-limit circuit ([[REQ-0003]]) independent of firmware behavior.

[[ECO-0001]] added the lead-disconnection fault injection step; before it
the procedure covered only a firmware-commanded overrange attempt.
