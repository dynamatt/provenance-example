---
id: VER-0001
type: VerificationProtocol
title: Bench verification of closed-loop amplitude adjustment
status: approved
verifies: [REQ-0001]
device_under_test: [CMP-0001]
equipment_needed: [EQP-0001, EQP-0002]
steps:
  - description: >-
      Place the implant in the saline tank, connect its sense electrode to
      the bench ECAP simulator and clip the precision current probe onto the
      stimulation lead. Program a fixed amplitude of 3 mA in open loop.
    acceptance_criteria: >-
      The simulator detects an evoked response on every stimulation cycle,
      and the probe reads 3 mA ± 2 %.
  - description: >-
      Configure the target band from the clinician settings and enable closed-loop
      control.
    acceptance_criteria: >-
      The implant reports closed-loop mode, and the target band it reads back
      matches the band configured.
  - description: >-
      Replay response profile 1, posture change from supine to standing,
      for 10 minutes.
    acceptance_criteria: >-
      The evoked response is within the target band on at least 99 % of
      cycles, and delivered amplitude stays within the configured range.
  - description: >-
      Replay response profile 2, a slow increase in neural sensitivity, for
      10 minutes.
    acceptance_criteria: >-
      The evoked response is within the target band on at least 99 % of
      cycles, and delivered amplitude stays within the configured range.
  - description: >-
      Replay response profile 3, transient responses from coughing, for 10
      minutes.
    acceptance_criteria: >-
      The evoked response returns to the target band within 5 cycles of each
      transient, and delivered amplitude stays within the configured range.
---
Apply a simulated ECAP response via bench stimulator, confirm the algorithm
under test ([[DES-0001]]) adjusts delivered amplitude to hold the response
within the configured target band across three representative response
profiles.
