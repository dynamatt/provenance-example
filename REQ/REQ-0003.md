title: Amplitude ceiling — subsystem allocation
status: draft

The pulse-generator ASIC shall enforce a hard current-limit circuit,
independent of firmware, as the subsystem-level implementation of the
system-level ceiling in [[REQ-0002]], so the ceiling holds under the
single-fault conditions of [[REF-0002]].
