---
id: REQ-0002
type: Requirement
title: Single-fault amplitude ceiling
status: approved
implements: [USR-0002]
verified_by: [VER-0002]
order: 2
---
The system shall not deliver a stimulation amplitude exceeding 8 mA under
any single-fault condition, including loss of the sense-electrode feedback
signal used by the closed-loop control algorithm ([[DES-0001]]).
