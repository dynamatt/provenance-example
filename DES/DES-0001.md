A control loop running on the implant's microcontroller that reads the
evoked compound action potential (ECAP) amplitude from the sense electrode
once per stimulation cycle and adjusts the delivered pulse amplitude to hold
the ECAP response within a clinician-configured target band, subject to the
hardware current-limit described in [[REQ-0003]].

Each cycle moves the amplitude toward the target band by a fixed fraction
of the error, as [[#control-law]] states, then clamps it to the ceiling:

```text
A[n+1] = min( A[n] + k · (N1_target − N1[n]),  A_max )
```
